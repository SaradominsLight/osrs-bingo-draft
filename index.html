What the numbers mean

Rank (#) and Value come from the sign-up formula. Iron is Ironman. QPC is the quest point cape; "New quests" means the sheet says no, new quests left. CB is combat level. Avail is availability from 1 to 6, 6 is most available. Skill is 1 to 10, 10 is grandmaster. Bank is bank value, 1 to 6. Region is the player's timezone. Tiers: T1 is Prom's alt through Wade, T2 is Moooooooo11 through Fe Morp, T3 is Barrott93 through CMoney, T4 is everyone after that. Click a column heading to sort.

Add a player

Click the + beside a player to draft them into the highlighted slot. Hover a drafted slot for their stats. If you are running the draft: click any empty slot to draft out of order, click a filled slot to send that player back to the pool, and press Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on Mac) to undo. Drag the bar under the board to resize it.
